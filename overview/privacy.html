Overview Uploader · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Overview Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Overview (@OverviewGeo) to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it uses

Through the YouTube Data API, the tool uploads the owner's video files, thumbnails and captions, sets their title, description, tags, playlist and publication time, and reads the owner's channel ID to make sure it is uploading to the right channel.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. No YouTube data or Google user data is sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact
